About AIR SPACE

Apa itu? Air Space Roleplay

Air Space Roleplay Adalah server SA-MP / San Andreas Multiplayer, dimana kalian bisa membuat karakter dan menghidupkan karakter kalian seperti di dunia nyata mulai dari bekerja, jadi polisi, jadi pemerintahan, bahkan kalian bisa melakukan Pekerjaan Ilegal dan bergabung dengan gang atau mafia.

What is Air Space Roleplay?

Air Space Roleplay is a SA-MP / San Andreas Multiplayer server where you can create a character and live their life as in the real world, ranging from working, being a police officer, being a government official, and even engaging in illegal activities and joining a gang or mafia.
